## App Information

- App name: OtoRec
- Platform: macOS
- Business model: Free app with one-time OtoRec Pro purchase
- Ads: None
- Analytics: None
- Account registration: Not required

## Common Checks

- Make sure macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission is enabled for
  OtoRec.
- Free recordings are limited to 5 minutes.
- OtoRec Pro unlocks unlimited recording, system audio recording, window
  capture, 60fps / HEVC, custom save destinations, and menu-bar recording start.
